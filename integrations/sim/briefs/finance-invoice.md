# Finance: Invoice preparation brief

Collect approved commercial and tax inputs before invoice preparation.

Input fields: objective (required text), sourceRefs (text), reviewer (text), department (text).

Client brief: Legal entity, client, signed terms, milestone, currency, line items and tax treatment approved by Finance.

Output sections: Billing parties and references; Approved line items; Tax treatment to confirm; Payment terms; Finance approval before issue.

The JSON starter contains Start -> Function, with a terminal structured template result. It has no model call, source retrieval or external action. Import it as a draft, inspect and run a synthetic sample, then deploy privately and map the ID in Superbrain PM.
