# Consulting Delivery: Training needs analysis pack

Client brief: Business outcomes, audience, current capability gaps, constraints and signed scope reference.

Output sections: Business outcomes; Audience and capability questions; Discovery methods; Programme design inputs; Success measures; Scope and reviewer.

The JSON starter contains Start -> Function, with a terminal structured template result. It has no model call, source retrieval or external action.
